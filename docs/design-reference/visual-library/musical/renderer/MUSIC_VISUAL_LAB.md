# Music Visual Lab — Required Gate

The normalized runtime SVGs are **candidates**, not production-approved assets, until this lab validates their scale, anchors and renderer integration.

## Required fixtures

1. Glyph gallery: all glyphs at 100%, 50%, 25% reference scale.
2. Treble clef placement: validate `gLine` against G4/second line.
3. Notehead family: open/filled optical scale consistency.
4. Pitch ladder: C4 through A5, all 13 positions present.
5. Extended ledger lines: A3/B3/C4/D4 and G5/A5/B5/C6/D6/E6.
6. Stem directions: below, on and above middle line.
7. Eighth/sixteenth isolated flags, including validated down-direction transform or separate asset decision.
8. Beamed motifs: every composer whitelist pattern, including hooks for `S16_E8_S16`.
9. Accidentals on lines and spaces.
10. Key signatures for `fifths=-7..+7`, generated by renderer, with deterministic spacing.
11. Straight and curved staff equivalence.
12. Ordinary/final barline on straight and curved ScorePath.
13. Composer gallery across fixed seeds and density profiles.

## Approval requirements

- no path geometry modification during calibration
- all metrics expressed relative to staffSpace
- required anchors populated in manifest
- deterministic fixtures
- human visual review in light and dark modes
- screenshot evidence stored outside runtime assets

Only after these gates may each asset `runtimeStatus` become `approved`.
