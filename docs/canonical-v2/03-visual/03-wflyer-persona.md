# W_Flyer Persona

## Purpose

Represent both the owner and the W_Flyer brand without showing or reconstructing the owner’s physical appearance.

## Approved direction

- Concept D, humanized;
- clearly humanoid silhouette;
- hooded coat integrated into brand geometry;
- non-photorealistic and non-realistic;
- minimal/abstract face treatment;
- W/F/logo angles structurally influence torso/coat/shoulders rather than appearing as a printed logo;
- professional, adult proportions; not chibi, superhero, robot, AI assistant, anime character, or childish mascot.

## Required rig poses

- `Neutral`
- `Working`
- `Presenting`
- `Peeking`

The final master/rig is a pending human-approved asset. Codex must not invent final geometry.

## Required About usage

About always contains the Persona. Reduced motion renders the final static pose.

## Optional easter eggs

- session-seeded selection, not per-render randomness;
- approximately 20–25% chance per eligible chapter;
- maximum 2 per session;
- minimum 2 chapters apart;
- no repeat in the same chapter;
- approximately 2–4 seconds;
- no required interaction;
- outside the master story timeline;
- deterministic dev debug control.

## Exclusions

No optional appearance:

- while a user types in Contact;
- while APP-04 is `PLAYING`;
- in Access W_Flyer;
- while a modal/dialog is open;
- when reduced motion disables animated easter eggs.

## Acceptance

No layout shift, focus theft, interaction obstruction, content replacement, or physical-likeness inference.
