# persona-easter-eggs Specification

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Approved Persona only
About SHALL contain the owner-approved hooded humanized Concept-D Persona. Codex SHALL NOT invent final geometry.

#### Scenario: Final Persona asset is unavailable
- **WHEN** the owner-approved final Persona asset has not been supplied
- **THEN** About exposes only its structural integration contract and no invented substitute geometry

### Requirement: Optional appearances are bounded
Optional appearances SHALL be session-seeded, maximum two per session, minimum two chapters apart, and excluded during Contact typing, APP-04 PLAYING, Access W_Flyer, modal/dialog, and reduced-motion animation suppression.

#### Scenario: An exclusion is active
- **WHEN** an optional Persona appearance is considered during any excluded state
- **THEN** the appearance is not shown and no required content or interaction is affected
