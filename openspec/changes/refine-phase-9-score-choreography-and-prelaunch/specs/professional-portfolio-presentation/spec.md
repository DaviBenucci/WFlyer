## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Professional choreography follows scene purpose
The Professional branch SHALL use a low About corridor with an explicit future-Persona exclusion, a shared card interaction behind Services, a restrained Process curve, a three-card Projects visitation, and a low gentle Contact wave. Foreground content and interactive controls MUST retain readability, pointer access, and keyboard access.

#### Scenario: About reserves future Persona space
- **WHEN** About is rendered before Persona implementation
- **THEN** the path avoids heading, body, CTA, and the explicit `PERSONA_RESERVED_ZONE` while no Persona asset or behavior is introduced

#### Scenario: Services participates without covering content
- **WHEN** the Services interaction approaches, reaches, and leaves maximum spread
- **THEN** the shared grammar begins before the first service card, progressively expands and attenuates behind every foreground card and text node, remains event-free in all non-canonical geometry, and returns to canonical staff only after the last card

#### Scenario: Process remains restrained
- **WHEN** Process is rendered
- **THEN** one long lower-to-middle gentle curve avoids its heading and stages and prepares the path for Projects without an expanded-card effect

#### Scenario: Contact calms the branch
- **WHEN** Contact is rendered in any projection mode
- **THEN** a low gentle wave avoids the heading, copy, links, every form control, consent, security widget, submit action, and live feedback

### Requirement: Project visits follow rendered cards
Professional Projects SHALL expose three deterministic visit anchors derived from the actual rendered project-card bounds. Each anchor MUST produce a visually recognizable score relationship beside, below, or behind its card without crossing card text, and the three notation-safe shelves MUST be separated by true event-free connector valleys.

#### Scenario: Project layout changes responsively
- **WHEN** the rendered project-card bounds change within a supported horizontal viewport
- **THEN** all three visit anchors are recomputed from the new bounds while visit order, event-safety, tangent limits on shelves, zero intersections, and the exit toward Contact remain intact

### Requirement: Immersive terminal owns the visual close
The immersive landing SHALL present the branch terminal as the closing/footer experience without rendering a second redundant visual footer. Standalone and internal routes MUST retain their shared footer behavior, and vertical/mobile reading MUST retain one discoverable semantic footer/navigation close.

#### Scenario: Professional desktop terminal
- **WHEN** the Professional terminal is visible on the immersive horizontal story
- **THEN** its shared footer navigation is present and no separate global visual footer follows it

#### Scenario: Standalone route footer
- **WHEN** a route outside the immersive story is rendered
- **THEN** its existing shared site footer remains available and unchanged by the immersive-only suppression
